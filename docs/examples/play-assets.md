# Asset ledger for the display sample

The names, lines, maps and pictures of `examples/play` were created new for this repository as a general-audience example. No pictures, sounds or lines are taken from other works. Code and text are MIT; pictures are CC BY 4.0 (`examples/play/game/assets/LICENSE`). The Go Regular font notice is in [ASSETS.md](../../ASSETS.md). For running and shooting the sample, see [Wayfinder](wayfinder.md).

`assets/walker/eyes_closed.png` and `mouth_open.png` are the difference pictures that animate Walker's talk portrait (eyes closed, mouth open). They are CC BY 4.0 like the other pictures. How they are placed is in `assets/walker/puppet.yaml` ([moving portraits](../architecture/puppet.md)).

The streamer picture and its differences in `examples/puppet/assets/streamer` (eyes closed, two mouth shapes, a surprised face) were also made for this repository as general-audience pictures, under CC BY 4.0 (`examples/puppet/assets/LICENSE`). `docs/images/puppet.gif` was shot from that example with `--tour --frames`.
