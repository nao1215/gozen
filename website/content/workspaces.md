These screens need schema 10 or later. The pictures come from the office sample
(`gozen run examples/office/game` in an engine checkout: an app team's sprint, six
people, three rooms, CC0 1.0 art listed in `examples/office/ASSETS.md`); the fixture
`e2e/atago/fixtures/workboard` uses a geometric picture. The calendar is in
[planning](../planning/).

A workspace combines a directory, a calendar from the game data and a burnup strip.
Browsing controls change only view variables, never the calendar.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
screens:
  studio:
    title: Studio
    kind: workspace
    view:
      fullscreen: true
      burnup:
        bounds: [380, 610, 500, 66]
        compact: true
        title: Progress
        total: Scope
        planned_label: Planned
        observed: Reported
    entries:
      - label: Potter
        bounds: [32, 180, 250, 350]
        portrait: true
        icon: portraits/potter.png
        muted: '!entity.potter.available'
        text: '{entity.potter.schedule}'
```

The entity and its observations are declared in `planning`. Portrait mode centers
a large face above the label; a muted portrait keeps its action. The compact burnup
reads the same observations as the full chart. Bounds are logical pixels, at least
120 by 48.

## State-dependent conversations

Pick a line with ordinary `if` branches, ordering overlapping conditions yourself.
Keep relationship values in variables or hidden planning attributes and show replies
and dates instead of the value.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
events:
  greeting:
    do:
      - if: var.energy < 20
        then: [{say: {who: scout, text: "Let us take a break."}}]
        else:
          - if: flag.returned
            then: [{say: {who: guide, text: "Welcome back."}}]
            else: [{say: {who: scout, text: "Ready to explore?"}}]
```

With materials on, entries and panels use `ui.material.surfaces.card`, otherwise a
solid fallback. Entry portraits scale up to a 36-unit radius within the row height;
`portrait: true` gets the larger profile area.

## Photo cards

![The office standup: busts, meters, dots, signs and a burnup](/images/workboard-cards.jpg)

<!-- Capture placement: workboard-cards.jpg, generated by make docs-images. -->

`picture` fills a bounded entry above a caption (the label and two text lines),
cropped to cover, so supply pictures with the card's aspect. The bounds need a height
of at least 120.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
entries:
  - label: Visitor
    text: Brings clay on Fridays.
    bounds: [32, 150, 300, 420]
    picture: pics/visitor-card.png
    do: [{set: {var.visitor_open: true}}]
```

## Reading and pressing

`style: plate` is for information: a frosted board with no edge, shadow or focus
stop. `style: button` is for an action: a pill with a bright edge, a centered label
and an accent ring when focused (put marks such as a chevron in the label). The
checker refuses a plate with `do` or a `destination` and a button without one.
Entries without a style keep the ordinary card.

## Ticket cards

![Tickets joined by a dependency line](/images/workboard-tickets.jpg)

<!-- Capture placement: workboard-tickets.jpg, generated by make docs-images. -->

`ticket` draws a bounded entry as a work ticket: the label is the title and `icon`
the portrait of whoever holds it.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
entries:
  - label: '{activity.fire.name}'
    bounds: [340, 200, 290, 150]
    icon: '{entity[activity.fire.assignee].portrait}'
    destination: ticket
    ticket:
      due: '{activity.fire.deadline}'
      state: '{activity.fire.observed >= activity.fire.effort ? "done" : "open"}'
      planned: '0.5'                                    # expected by now, 0..1
      progress: 'activity.fire.observed / activity.fire.effort'
      behind: 'planning.date > activity.fire.deadline'
      blocked: 'activity.glaze.observed < activity.glaze.effort'
      after: ['{activity.glaze.name}']
```

The bar fills to `progress` with a mark at `planned`. `behind` colors the stripe, bar
and due date as late; `blocked` grays them and puts a lock before the names in
`after`. A visible ticket whose label is in `after` is joined by a line (an arrow
between neighbors on a row, otherwise over their tops). Empty names are dropped, so a
repeated entry can list optional prerequisites.

## Carousels

![The help carousel turning to an unavailable choice](/images/workboard-carousel.mp4)

<!-- Capture placement: workboard-carousel.mp4, generated by make docs-images. -->

Bounded actions with the same `carousel` name form one rotating selector of square
boxes showing `picture` (or the label's first letter), the chosen one large in the
middle. Left, right, the arrow buttons or a click on a side box turn it; OK or a
click on the middle confirms; up and down treat it as one stop. Under the middle box
are its label, `cost` as a chip and its text. The middle box is the band height less
86, from 48 to 180.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
entries:
  - label: Fire
    carousel: tools
    picture: icons/fire.png
    cost: 1 slot
    text: Fire the kiln.
    bounds: [320, 500, 928, 196]
    do: [{call: fire}]
  - label: Glaze
    carousel: tools
    picture: icons/glaze.png
    text: Glaze a bowl.
    unavailable: 'var.kiln_busy'
    reason: The kiln is busy today.
    bounds: [320, 500, 928, 196]
    do: [{call: glaze}]
```

A screen has one carousel at most; its members share bounds at least 120 high, and it
replaces the screen's bottom band. `unavailable` works on any bounded action: the
control stays faded, confirming does nothing (the trace records `screen_refused`),
`reason` replaces its text, a script `pick` skips it and `look` lists it as disabled.

## Dragging and dropping

![Acceptance tests is refused by Daniel, then Payments is handed to him](/images/workboard-drag.mp4)

On a full-screen surface an entry with `drag` can be carried onto one with `drop`.
`drag` is interpolated into the value handed over (empty is not carried). `drop`
names a parameter: a drop runs the place's `do` with `arg.<name>` bound to the value
as a string. `accepts` refuses the drop while false; `hint` shows on the place while
something is carried.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
entries:
  - label: '{activity[arg.id].name}'
    each: {param: id, items: [glaze]}
    bounds: [40, 150, 280, 150]
    drag: '{arg.id}'
    ticket: {due: '{activity[arg.id].deadline}', progress: 'activity[arg.id].observed / activity[arg.id].effort'}
  - label: Potter
    icon: '{entity.potter.portrait}'
    bounds: [420, 150, 300, 140]
    drop: job
    accepts: 'activity[arg.job].assignee != "potter"'
    hint: 'Give {activity[arg.job].name}'
    do: [{plan: {op: assign, activity: '{arg.job}', entity: potter}}]
```

- Mouse and touch: move a pressed entry 6 pixels to drag a translucent copy, and let
  go over a place to drop or elsewhere to put it down. A press without moving picks
  it up; then press a place to drop, the entry or empty space to put it down, or
  another entry to carry that one.
- Keys and pads: up and down also stop on entries and places; OK picks up or drops,
  cancel puts down.
- The carried entry gets an accent ring, a place that takes it an accent edge, a place
  that refuses it a fade and a cross.
- A drop runs the place like any action. The trace records `screen_carry`,
  `screen_drop` and `screen_refused` with `screen`, `entry` and `source` (the carried
  value). A refresh keeps the carried entry by its entry and row arguments, or puts it
  down when it is gone.
- A script picks entries and places by label (a place is disabled while nothing it takes is carried); `look` adds `held`, the carried label.
  `drag X Y TO_X TO_Y` presses, moves over eight ticks and lets go.

The carried value is not in scope in `label`, `text`, `icon`, `when`, `muted`,
`unavailable` or `reason`, which are drawn while nothing is carried. A carried entry
has no `do`, `destination`, `carousel`, `target`, `drop` or `peek`; a place needs
`do` and has no `destination`, `carousel`, `target` or `peek`.

## Peeks

![Daniel's peek opening and closing on the team screen](/images/workboard-peek.mp4)

<!-- Capture placement: workboard-peek.mp4, generated by make docs-images. -->

Pressing an entry with `peek` opens its text in a small glass panel beside it,
running nothing. A calendar day plate is the usual case:

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
- {label: '7/31', style: plate, peek: 'Kiln maintenance in the evening.', bounds: [800, 150, 160, 80]}
```

OK opens it from the keys; any press or cancel closes it and does nothing else. A script `pick` opens it and
`look` adds `peek`, the open text; the trace records `screen_peek` with `open`.

## Status dots

`dot` draws a small circle of an interpolated `#rrggbb` in the top-right corner of an
entry. Pair it with a word so color is not the only sign; an unreadable color draws
nothing.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
- {label: Ann, style: plate, text: 'Tired', dot: '{var.ann_tired ? "#e8a07a" : "#7fc8a9"}', bounds: [32, 150, 200, 120]}
```

## Busts and meters

`bust` draws a head-to-chest crop of a standing picture at the left of the entry in
place of the icon, placed by its face box (assets/faces.json). `width` is the share
of the entry (default .4); `top` (where the face starts) and `face` (its height) are
fractions of the crop's height (defaults .12 and .3). Muted entries veil it gray.

`meter` draws a ticket's bar along the bottom: `progress` (0..1), `planned` and
`behind` as on a ticket.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
- label: Ann
  style: plate
  bust: {picture: 'portraits/ann/base.png', width: .4, top: .14, face: .42}
  meter: {progress: 'var.ann_done', planned: 'var.ann_plan', behind: 'var.ann_done < var.ann_plan'}
  muted: '!var.ann_here'
  bounds: [32, 150, 296, 222]
```

## Signs

`sign` (an interpolated path) draws a small square picture left of the `dot`, such
as an office, a house or a moon for where a person is today.

## Messages

![Elena's message is opened and a choice appears](/images/workboard-message.mp4)

The `message` command queues a chat message that asks for a decision:

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
- message: {who: ann, text: 'The kiln is late. Shall I stay tonight?', event: kiln_late}
```

`event` is a parameterless event, usually a `choice`; `tag` puts a short mark such as
`DM` on it. Queuing does not stop the current event.

On a full-screen surface (not a video call) the oldest message is a notification at
the top right: face, name, first line and how many others wait. Pressing it or the
inbox key (I) opens a bubble with the whole text and runs the event there, its choice
under the bubble. Unopened messages are saved (`inbox`, at most 50, oldest dropped).
A script picks the notification by its label (the translated word for message, a
colon and the name); `look` adds `inbox` and `message`; the trace records
`message_open`. Under `ui.material` the notification, the bubble and guide lines sit
on a card at least 0.94 opaque.

## Call view

![The team call, then Elena shares the booking form](/images/workboard-call.mp4)

![Elena shares the booking form](/images/workboard-share.jpg)

`call_view` changes how a video call looks without changing state:

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
- say: {text: (Ann turns her camera on.), ensemble: studio}
- call_view: {camera: {who: ann, on: true}}
```

The override lasts until the first line outside a call. Narration and the caller's
lines keep the last speaker on the stage; the caller's lines also light the self view.

`call_view: {share: {who, picture}}` draws the picture (under `assets/`) whole above
the message window in place of the speaker, with the sharer's name and a share mark;
participants stay in their strip or column. It shows only on calls the sharer is in
and ends with an empty picture or the first line outside a call.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
- call_view: {share: {who: ann, picture: share/plan.png}}
- say: {who: ann, text: This is the plan., ensemble: studio}
- call_view: {share: {who: ann, picture: ""}}
```

A `choice` after a call line with `muted: {seconds, do}` shows a struck red mic and
an Unmute button; unmuting brings the answers, and after `seconds` the muted `do`
runs instead (see [Events](../writing-a-game/#events)).

## Full call

A call can fill the screen under the ordinary message window:

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
view:
  call:
    address: call.example.test/studio
    layout: full        # the browser maximized over the whole screen
    captions: overlay   # the message window over the call, down to the lower edge
    others: side        # the participants not speaking as round faces in the side column
    others_title: People
    window_opacity: 0.8 # a deeper window over the moving pictures
```

The window keeps its usual top and width, so lines wrap as elsewhere; only its lower
edge moves. The person on the stage is framed above the window, and the side list,
controls and self view stand over it. `others: side` also works without
`layout: full`, replacing the chat transcript. Joins and leaves show as faces coming
and going, and `chat.reaction` shows under the name. A call of two folds the column
for every line: the stage widens, the controls take a slim right column, and during
`self_stage` the participant moves to the small view.

A picture or non-guest self view (`self: me.png`) stands on the plain tile;
`self_backdrop: room.png` puts a picture behind it (entities keep their own backdrop,
guests their `guest_backdrops`). Faces on camera are framed by the detected face and
move down, then shrink, when hair or a hat would leave the view. A picture opaque to
its top edge is drawn cut there.

Lines shared by several calls name no `ensemble`; the caller names the screen, and
the called events show over that call:

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
- choice:
  - label: Talk about the scope
    do: [{call: {event: scope_talk, ensemble: client_call}}]
```
## Face shape

Faces on planning screens are round. `ui.face_shape: square` makes them rounded
squares, and a screen's `view.face_shape` (round or square) overrides it. A video
call (`view.call`) stays round.

## Flows

![The day flow's steps in the header](/images/workboard-flow.mp4)

<!-- Capture placement: workboard-flow.mp4, generated by make docs-images. -->

`flows` in game.yaml puts full-screen surfaces in a fixed order.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
flows:
  day:
    steps:
      - {label: Progress, screen: board_progress, enter: ask_about_late}
      - {label: People, screen: board_people}
      - {label: Actions, screen: board_actions}
    restart: '"" + planning.date + planning.phase'
    skip: 'var.runs > 0'
    next: 'Next ›'
```

- The header band shows the current step in the accent, finished steps with a check
  (press to go back) and later ones locked unless `skip` holds.
- Every step but the last has a next action, 176 by 48 at the bottom right (16 from
  the right, 24 from the bottom, left of any tabs), drawn over the entries, so keep
  that corner free. It replaces the screen with the next step without stacking.
  `next` is its label (default Next › in English).
- Opening a step not reached yet opens the current step, unless `skip` holds.
- `restart` is a pure value; when it changes, the flow starts over, passing steps whose
  `done` holds or whose `when` leaves them out.
- `enter` is a parameterless event run the first time a step shows under each restart
  value.
- Progress is saved (`flows.<name>`: `reached`, `key`, `entered`). The trace records
  `flow_next` and `flow_enter`. Scripts pick steps by label ("2 People"); `look` adds
  `flow`, `step` and `reached` (from 1).

Steps are parameterless full-screen surfaces in one flow only; a flow has 2 to 10.

Steps that time finishes are held, and steps for some days have a `when`:

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
flows:
  day:
    steps:
      - {label: Calendar, screen: today}
      - {label: Meeting, screen: standup, when: '!planning.closed'}
      - {label: Work, screen: desk, hold: true, done: 'planning.phase != "morning"', when: '!planning.closed'}
      - {label: Evening, screen: home}
    restart: 'planning.date'
```

- A held step (`hold: true` with `done`, not the last) has no next action; only `done`
  finishes it, after which it and earlier steps stay closed under that restart value.
- A step screen that no longer opens as itself after an action is replaced by the
  step that does, without stacking.
- `when` (a pure condition, not on the first step) keeps a step while it holds;
  otherwise the step is passed and left out of the band's numbering.

## First-visit guides

![Martin's first-visit guide over the veiled standup](/images/workboard-guide.mp4)

<!-- Capture placement: workboard-guide.mp4, generated by make docs-images. -->

A screen's `guide` walks a new player through it once, in a character's voice.

<!-- verify: parse screen or command fragment; combine with the neutral workboard definitions -->
```yaml
screens:
  board_progress:
    entries:
      - {label: 'Timeline ›', style: button, mark: timeline, bounds: [32, 620, 200, 38], do: [...]}
    guide:
      when: 'planning.phase == "morning"'
      steps:
        - {who: lead, text: 'Reports first. The line under the plan is where we really are.'}
        - {who: lead, text: 'Fine. On to the people.', focus: [next]}
```

- Lines show one by one in the notification corner with the speaker's face; chat
  notifications wait.
- `focus` names `mark`s of bounded entries (rows of an `each` may share one) or
  `next`. Everything else is veiled and refuses presses, cancel, save and load. The
  line moves on when a lit place acts, or with `until` when its condition holds.
  Without `focus`, any press or OK moves on.
- A finished guide is stored as `guide:<screen>` in gallery.json and never shows
  again. Scripted, replayed and automatic plays skip guides unless the script says
  `guides on`; the trace has `guide`, `guide_blocked` and `guide_done`.

`view.header_face` (an interpolated picture path) puts a small face after the date,
and `view.header_tint` (an interpolated `#rrggbb`) rings it.

## Scene ordering and voice diagnostics

Reservation `after`, `exclusive` and `when`, actor `speech` checks, call sizes and
save migration are in the [planning author guide](../planning/).

## Entry layout contract

Plates and plain entries render `picture`, `picture_fit`, `bust`, `meter`, `dot` and
`sign`. Buttons render label, text and tone only. Tickets render their bar and icon.
Carousels take pictures and busts (`contain` too) but not meters, dots or signs.
`gozen check` rejects the rest.

## Inspect pictures and carried cards

A script's `look` reports `on`, `controls`, `disabled` and, when present,
`pictures`, `held` and `peek`. `shot` captures pixels only in `run`, not in
`sim script`.

## Profiles and quick controls

![The character book in a grid](/images/workboard-profiles.jpg)

`ui.profile_book: {layout: grid}` shows unlocked profiles in a grid (`--dev` reveals
all of them). `ui.quick_menu: below` puts the dialogue controls under the message
window and `inside` within it; `ui.novel_window` positions the window. Phase looks in
the calendar change the background without presentation commands. Defaults are in
the [schema](../schema/).

## Recording and performance

The capture recipes are in `docs/images/manifest.yaml` and use `examples/office`;
run `make docs-images` under Xvfb on Linux. A full-screen save needs a finished event
or checkpoint, so close the workspace before saving. Guide completion lives in the
gallery, not saves; scripted runs with `guides on` keep no gallery unlocks.

## Overlapping materials

![Two overlapping glass panels over the office](/images/workboard-materials.jpg)

Panels sample the layer under them, earlier panels included (the fixture's
`materials` screen overlaps two). `ui.material.surfaces` picks mica, acrylic or glass
per surface role.

<!-- verify: parse presentation fragment; merge into the fixture's game.yaml -->
```yaml
ui:
  profile_book: {layout: grid}
  quick_menu: below
planning:
  calendar:
    looks:
      morning: {label: Morning, time: '09:00', color: '#ffe8c0', wash: 0.15, card: true}
```

Phase looks tint the workspace and can show a transition card.
